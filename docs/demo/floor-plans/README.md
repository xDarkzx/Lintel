# Demo floor plans

Two levels of a real building for trying the Maps page: the Contagious Disease
Hospital Office Building on Ellis Island, New York, cropped to the building.

| File | Source sheet |
|---|---|
| `ground-floor.png` | [First Floor Plan, sheet 3 of 9](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/ny2080.sheet.00003a) |
| `level-1.png` | [Second Floor Plan, sheet 4 of 9](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/ny2080.sheet.00004a) |

Credit: Historic American Buildings Survey (HABS NY-6086-M), National Park
Service, Ellis Island Documentation Project, 2009; delineated by Luis Pieraldi
and Paul Davidson. Public domain as a work of the US federal government
(Library of Congress, via Wikimedia Commons).

In the console, add a floor under Maps, choose **Upload plan**, then
**Edit layout** to drag doors onto it.
